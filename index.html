02 · Services

Digital transformation

Paper forms, shared inboxes and spreadsheets replaced with systems of record. We inventory the documents, agree what each one is actually for, and retire the ones nobody reads.

  • Process and document audit
  • Systems selection and integration
  • Migration off paper and PDFs

Process automation

The repetitive middle of a workflow — intake, routing, approval chasing, re-keying — handed to software, with exceptions escalated to a named person rather than swallowed.

  • Workflow and approval routing
  • Document intake and extraction
  • Exception handling and alerting

Consulting & training

Advice and hands-on training so the system keeps running after we leave. We write the runbook, train the operators, and hand over the source.

  • Technical due diligence
  • Operator and admin training
  • Handover, runbooks and source
03 · AI capability

Automation you can audit

We use language models where they measurably remove manual effort — reading documents, classifying requests, drafting the routine reply — and nowhere else. Every model step is bounded by a lever a human can pull: a confidence threshold, a review queue, a log, a rollback.

That is the same discipline we apply to our own practice. Automating our internal delivery work is why a small team can carry engagements of this size at this price — and it is the capability we hand to clients rather than describe.

  • 01Integration with Copilot, ChatGPT and comparable platforms already licensed in your business
  • 02Domain-tuned assistants grounded in your own documents and terminology
  • 03Context-aware copilots inside the software your team already opens each morning
  • 04Evaluation harnesses, so a model change is a measured change and not a surprise

04 · How we work
01

Review

A short, scoped engagement to start. We sit with the people doing the work and document the process as it really runs.

02

Scope

A written scope with a fixed price, the assumptions it rests on, and what we would not build. You can take it to another firm.

03

Build

Short increments, something usable at the end of each. Automation ships behind its levers, in review mode, before it runs on its own.

04

Hand over

Source, runbook and training to your team. Ongoing support is a separate agreement, not a dependency we design in.
