03 · AI capability

Automation you can audit

We use language models where they measurably remove manual effort — reading documents, classifying requests, drafting the routine reply — and nowhere else. Every model step is bounded by a lever a human can pull: a confidence threshold, a review queue, a log, a rollback.

That is the same discipline we apply to our own practice. Automating our internal delivery work is why a small team can carry engagements of this size at this price — and it is the capability we hand to clients rather than describe.

05 · About

A very small team, on purpose

Paperdrop was formed by product managers, trainers, designers, architects and developers with fifty years of combined industry experience. We work with small and midsize businesses whose processes still run on paper and attachments — work that is slow, error-prone and more expensive than it looks on the ledger.

A conventional delivery team is a chain of verticals: analysis hands to design, design to build, build to test, test to documentation. Every handover costs time and loses detail. We use AI to collapse that chain. The same few people carry an engagement from process review to runbook, with language models doing the drafting, scaffolding, test generation and documentation that used to need a separate role each.

That is why we stay very small and take fewer engagements than we could. The people you meet in the review are the people who build the thing, and there is nobody in between.

06 · Product
Beredo

Set your process up once. Run it forever.

Beredo is the product we built for firms that collect documents from clients. A mortgage application, a tax return, a new-client onboarding: the same steps, the same documents, the same signatures, every time — and every time rebuilt by hand in somebody's inbox. The work is not hard. Keeping track of it is.

You write the process down once: who does what, in what order. Every client after that runs on it. Each person is asked for one thing at a time, in the right order, and the firm stops carrying the process in its head.

  • 01Your clients never make an account. They follow a link from an email and do the one thing waiting on them.
  • 02It chases people so you do not have to, and tells somebody else if it keeps waiting.
  • 03Unlimited people on every plan. You are not charged for your own customers.
  • 04Built for mortgage brokers, accountants, immigration lawyers, property managers, recruiters, clinics and practices.

How it works
01

Build the flow

Steps, and the forms, uploads, signatures and approvals in each. Say who each one belongs to and what counts as done.

02

Start an application

One mortgage, one tax return, one onboarding. Invite the people involved; they need no account and no software, just the link they were emailed.

03

It runs

Each person sees the one thing waiting on them and nothing else. When they finish, the next thing opens for whoever it belongs to, and the firm can see where every application has got to.
